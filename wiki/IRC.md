Libreswan developers can be found on the
`#libreswan`
[IRC](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IRC) channel on
[Libera.Chat](https://libera.chat/).

IRC works best when you're able to remain online, and to do this you'll
need to install a local IRC client.  The thing is, we may not be online
when your comments are posted, and you may not be
online when we're ready to answer.

If you're not able to hang around then another medium, such as e-mail,
may be more effective.

There's an old saying, _Everything on the Internet Takes At Least A Week_; for
IRC give it 24-hours + weekends.
