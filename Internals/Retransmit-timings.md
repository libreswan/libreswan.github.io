The retransmit values are not fast enough for modern networks. Instead
of 20 or 40 seconds, it should be somewhere between 1-4 seconds (to be
discussed)

These should be configurable on a per-connection basis

These should be in milliseconds to allow greater precission.

Refined retransmission might would to improve battery life on hand held
devices.
