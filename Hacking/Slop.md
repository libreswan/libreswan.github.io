### The ChangeLog entries are Drival

*We don't need A.I. to tell us what was changed
in excruciating detail, we can see that by looking
at the patch.*

We're not asking for much, but we are looking
for a little Human touch.

you'll quickly pick up the style.  The key thing is to keep
it short.  If there's something complicated going on,
make it a comment in the code.  That way we'll see it.
