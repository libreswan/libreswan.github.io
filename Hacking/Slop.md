Here are the most common reasons for something being flagged as
`Slop`.

## Pull-Requests

*We're looking for improvements. If we wanted to turn Libreswan into
A.I. generated mush, we would do it ourselves.*

### The Summary or Rationale Wasn't Filled In

*A.I. submitted pull-requests seem to consistently skip this step.*

We're not asking for much, but we are looking for a Human touch.

Please provide us with a brief overview of what's happening, and
especially the motivation.

### The ChangeLog entries are Drival

*A.I. likes to rationalize what was changed in excruciatingly detailed
ChangeLogs that no one will read; anyone needing to know what changed
will look at the diff.*

We're not asking for much, but we are looking for a Human touch.

Read a few commit messages, you'll quickly pick up the style.  The key
thing is to keep it short.  If there's something complicated going on,
make it a comment in the code.  That way we'll see it when we read the
diff.

## Bugs

*Like a politician, A.I. is selective with the truth.*

### The Questions We ask Were Ignored

*If A.I. doesn't know what it doesn't know, how can we trust its
analysis.*

The bug reporting form has some very specific requests.  Notably, a
non-debug log of the entire exchange, so we can all understand the big
picture and figure out the root cause.
